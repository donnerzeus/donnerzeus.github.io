Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DonnerZeus ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Nexus Defender (the "Game").

1. Information stored on your device

Nexus Defender stores game progress, settings, earned currency, unlocked content, mission progress, consent choices, and limited gameplay diagnostic events locally on your device. This information allows the Game to save your progress and preferences.

We do not currently operate a player account system or a developer-controlled server that receives this locally stored game data. You can remove local data by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling the Game. Clearing local data may permanently remove your progress.

2. Advertising

Some versions of the Game may display rewarded or interstitial advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Rewarded advertisements are optional and provide an in-game benefit after completion. Interstitial advertisements may appear at appropriate breaks between game sessions.

When advertising is enabled, Google may automatically collect or process information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions with the Game and advertisements, diagnostic and performance information, advertising identifiers, app set identifiers, and other device or account identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required by law, the Game will request your privacy choices before serving personalized advertising. If you select limited or non-personalized advertising, ads may still be selected using contextual information and may still require limited data processing for delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

3. In-app purchases

Some versions of the Game may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Google processes payments. We do not receive or store your full payment card or bank account details. The Game may receive product identifiers, purchase status, transaction tokens, and entitlement information needed to deliver, restore, and verify purchased content and prevent fraud.

Purchases are also subject to Google Play's terms and privacy practices.

4. How information is used

Information may be processed to:

  • save progress, preferences, and consent choices;
  • operate, maintain, balance, and improve the Game;
  • deliver and measure advertisements where enabled;
  • complete and restore in-app purchases where enabled;
  • diagnose crashes, errors, and performance problems; and
  • protect players, the Game, and service providers from fraud or abuse.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only when their services are present and enabled in the installed version of the Game. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google Play Terms of Service

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, and prevent fraud.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall it. If you contact us, we may retain your message and contact details only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. Because Nexus Defender does not currently provide player accounts or cloud saves, there is no developer-hosted player profile to delete. You may contact us using the address below with a privacy or deletion request.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding personal information, or to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

You can manage advertising privacy settings and reset or delete your advertising identifier through your Android device settings where supported. You can also change available advertising consent choices within the Game. Device menu names may vary by Android version and manufacturer.

8. Children's privacy

Nexus Defender is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the Game and the information processed. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, such processing is subject to appropriate legal safeguards maintained by those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its service providers, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes may also be communicated within the Game or through the Google Play listing where appropriate.
